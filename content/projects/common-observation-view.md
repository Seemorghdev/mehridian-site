<section class="case-section" aria-labelledby="integration-problem"><div><p class="eyebrow">01 / The problem</p><h2 id="integration-problem">The inputs<br>do not agree.</h2></div><div class="body"><p>The original challenge concerned a common display for heterogeneous observations in an air-defence setting. This showcase documents the software artifacts at a high level; it does not expose the operational implementation or offer a live sensor system.</p><p>The engineering concern is easy to underestimate: similarly named fields can represent different things. A timestamp format, unit or locally assigned identifier cannot be interpreted safely without its source context.</p></div></section>
