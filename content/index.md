    <h2 id="research-title">A passing check is not an acceptance.</h2>
    <p>Fifteen repositories had accepted evidence packages in a historical comparison. The records preserve verification results and engineering decisions as different questions—not a single quality score.</p>
    <div class="work-inline-links"><a href="./research/project03/evidence-explorer.md">Explore the frozen comparison →</a><a href="./research/project03/index.md">Read the research account →</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="work-measures" aria-label="Separate frozen schema-v1.1 distributions">
    <div class="work-measure"><strong>13<span> / 15</span></strong><p>Repository candidates with verification <code>SUCCESS_OBSERVED</code>. One was <code>MIXED</code>; one <code>NOT_ESTABLISHED</code>.</p></div>
    <div class="work-measure"><strong>1<span> / 15</span></strong><p>Repositories with engineering acceptance <code>ESTABLISHED</code>. Thirteen were <code>NOT_ESTABLISHED</code>; one <code>UNKNOWN</code>.</p></div>
