# Publication and runtime reality

[[research/project03/index|← Project 03 Story Map]]

This is where ordinary language becomes dangerous. Public. Published. Live. Claimed live. Observed live.

They are close enough that my brain wants to compress them. The frozen model refuses to.

## What was observed

Across 25 accepted projection observations, runtime observation is:

- `ESTABLISHED`: 0
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`: 18
- `NOT_OBSERVED`: 4
- `NOT_COLLECTED`: 1
- `UNKNOWN`: 2

The easiest wrong sentence would be “none of the systems were live.” The evidence instead says no positive independent runtime observation was established in that projection set.

`NOT_COLLECTED` preserves a collection boundary. `UNKNOWN` preserves an unresolved state. Neither should silently become a failed runtime test.

## A claim is not an observation

The historical Showcase public-presentation record is `PUBLICLY_VISIBLE`, with runtime `LIVE_CLAIMED` and independent observation `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

The reason says the documented live surface could not be independently observed. It explicitly warns that this is not a runtime failure observation. Without that sentence, “I could not independently observe it” could easily become “it did not work.”

This note is not a status page for today's demonstrations. It also cannot certify that a claimed surface was unreachable: the historical source records a **claim** and the lack of a positive **independent observation**, not an observed failure.

## One repository can contain several different surfaces

Platform has seven accepted projection observations: current and legacy surfaces, public and not-public surfaces, explicit non-claims, no-claim-found states and a live claim. A single “live/not-live” repository label would destroy detail that the source retains.

Replication supplies another important boundary: its two keyed projection instances each preserve `EXPLICITLY_NOT_CLAIMED` with observation `UNKNOWN` and reason `RUNTIME_DISCOVERY_INCOMPLETE`. They must not be replaced with a portfolio-wide `NO_RUNTIME_CLAIM_FOUND` or `NOT_OBSERVED` label.

## What the zero means

The zero answers a scoped evidence question. It does not state that all 25 runtime surfaces were tested and found unavailable.

The zero tells me what Project 03 established independently. It does not tell me the full historical deployment reality, still less today's reality.

Access limits, intentionally offline surfaces, stale claims and observation methods are possible explanations, not measured causes. They remain open questions.
